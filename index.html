Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Golfo is built to keep your golf on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of our own, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your rounds, scores, profile (name, photo, handicap), clubs and the greens you place on the map are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Your location

With your permission, Golfo uses your location to find the course you're playing, show distances to the green and get the local weather. To do this, the area around your position is sent to:

  • OpenStreetMap data servers (Overpass) – to fetch the holes, greens and water on the course.
  • Open-Meteo – to fetch wind, temperature and elevation.
  • Apple Maps – to show the map and satellite imagery.

These requests are not linked to you and contain no name or account. The services may keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies. Location is only used while you use the app.

Coaching

The round analysis is written by Apple Intelligence directly on your iPhone. Your round is not sent anywhere for this.

Export

If you export your rounds as a CSV file, the file goes only where you choose to share it.

Children

Golfo does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Email support

No distances are shown

Golfo needs your location. Go to Settings → Golfo → Location and choose "While Using the App". Distances are only shown when you're on the course.

My course isn't found

Course maps come from OpenStreetMap, and not every course is mapped yet. Tap the middle of the green the first time you play each hole – Golfo remembers it for next time. You can also add your course on openstreetmap.org.

The green is in the wrong place

Tap "Move green" on the map and then tap the middle of the green.

Why is my handicap marked "EST."?

Without an official handicap, Golfo estimates one from your 18-hole rounds. It's not an official World Handicap System index. Enter your own handicap under Settings → Profile.

Can I use Golfo in a competition?

Turn on Competition mode in Settings or when you start a round. Golfo then shows only distances and the weather forecast, as the Rules of Golf allow. Check the competition's local rules too.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Data → Export data gives you all rounds as a CSV file.
